This page lists live, instructor-led workshops about napari: sessions taught
by the napari team from the [workshop catalogue](#home), and workshops
organized by the wider napari community.

napari team workshops run in small cohorts, in person at conferences and
online. Self-guided versions of every session are always freely available —
see the [workshop catalogue](#home) for the workshops and their session
lengths.

Interested in attending, hosting, or sponsoring a workshop?
Reach out on [Zulip](https://napari.zulipchat.com) or the
[community forum](https://forum.image.sc/tag/napari).

[this page](https://github.com/napari/workshops/blob/main/docs/events.md),
or let us know on [Zulip](https://napari.zulipchat.com). Once it has run, it
